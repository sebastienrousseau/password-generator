## Highlights ⭐️

* **Packaging & Distribution**: Bundles packages/core and TypeScript definitions into distribution artifacts, resolving module resolution crashes for consumers.
* **CLI Options & Ergonomics**: Adds --allowed-chars and --forbidden-chars for custom character sets, --reveal for honeywords, and automated 45-second clipboard scrubbing.
* **Entropy Precision**: Computes theoretical Shannon entropy in the CLI directly from generator configuration without character-pool inflation.
* **Vectorized CSPRNG Sampling**: Optimizes NodeCryptoRandom with batch byte generation and rejection sampling for higher throughput.
